Model settings

One-compartment oral model with first-order absorption and elimination (Bateman function), 90% bioavailability, volume of distribution 3.5 L/kg. Doses add together by superposition. It's a population-average estimate, not a measured blood level; urine pH alone can shift the real half-life by several hours. Everything is stored only in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere. For an NFC tag or shortcut, open this page's address with ?tap on the end: it logs 5 mg straight away and lets you change or delete it. ?dose=2.5 or ?dose=10 logs that amount instead. Use Export CSV now and then as a backup. CSV import also reads the older two-column format: time,dose_mg with times like 2026-10-06 09:50.
